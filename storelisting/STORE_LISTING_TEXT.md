## 3. Full Description (1,890 / 4,000 characters)
```text
Meet Geko — your smart, private, offline-first personal finance and budget tracker built for everyday Philippine wallets, banks, and spending habits.

Whether you pay with GCash, Maya, GoTyme, BPI, BDO, UnionBank, credit cards, or physical cash, Geko keeps all your balances, expenses, receipts, and savings goals organized in one fast, clean dashboard.

KEY FEATURES

• 100+ Philippine Cards & E-Wallets in 3D
Connect and track your balances across GCash, Maya, GoTyme Bank, BPI, BDO, Metrobank, UnionBank, RCBC, Security Bank, MariBank, SeaBank, Beep Card, Autosweep/Easytrip RFID, and physical cash with bespoke 3D card visuals.

• Chat Geko (Taglish Expense Logger & Financial Assistant)
Just type or speak naturally in English or Taglish—like "Kumain kami sa Chili's gamit ko GoTyme 700" or "Bumili ako ng facial wash gamit cash 300"—and Geko automatically categorizes the expense, records the transaction, and updates your card balance.

• Realtime Optical Receipt Scanner
Snap a photo of any thermal POS receipt, grocery bill, or e-wallet payment screenshot. Geko extracts the merchant name, line items, 12% VAT, and total amount due automatically.

• Interactive Expense Calendar & Deductions Explorer
Inspect your daily inflow, spending, and net cashflow on a visual monthly calendar stamped with Geko badges on active transaction days.

• Savings Goals, Salary Cutoff Planner & BNPL Installment Tracker
Plan around your 15th and 30th salary cutoffs, track buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) monthly installments, set category budget meters, and monitor credit card debt payoff in one place.

• 100% Private & Local-First
Your financial data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. No bank login credentials, no cloud sign-up, and no ads. Export or back up your records anytime to JSON or CSV.
```
